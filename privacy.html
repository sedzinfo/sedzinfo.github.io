Privacy

How the sites under sedzinfo.github.io use Google Analytics, and how to change your choice.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Who runs the sites

The sites under sedzinfo.github.io are run by Dimitrios Zacharatos. Contact: through GitHub, at github.com/sedzinfo.

Google Analytics

The sites use Google Analytics 4 only to count visits and see which pages are read, and only after you click Accept on the banner at the bottom of the page. Until then, Google Analytics is not loaded.

What is stored in your browser

NameTypePurpose
ga-consent localStorage Your choice (yes or no), saved when you click Accept or Reject. It is not a cookie. All the sites on the domain share it, so you answer once.
_ga
_ga_VSX11PYNWT		 cookies Set by Google Analytics, only after you accept.

Choosing Reject stops Google Analytics and removes these cookies.

Changing your choice

Use Cookie settings at the bottom of every page to open the banner again and change your choice at any time.

Google

Google processes the data that Google Analytics collects, and may process it outside the EU. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Separately from Google Analytics, the theme of the home page, this page and the vignettes sites (Bootswatch Flatly) loads the Lato font from Google Fonts when a page opens, whatever you choose on the banner.

Hosting

The sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The crypto app on shinyapps.io

The crypto app runs on shinyapps.io, outside this domain, with the same banner and Google Analytics as these sites. Because it is on another domain, it keeps its own ga-consent choice: you answer there separately, and change it with Cookie settings at the bottom of the app. The app is hosted by Posit; see the Posit privacy policy.
